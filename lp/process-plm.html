THE COMMON CHALLENGE

Changes in formula development shouldn't get lost in translation on their way to the rest of your organization

Most manufacturers of formulated, process-based products need a modern PLM system built for how formulation actually works, not email threads and shared drives, and not legacy PLM systems straining to keep up. Sound familiar?

Formulators live in spreadsheets, synced by hand

Every export, re-key, and version check is another chance for the formula and the spec to drift apart, and reformulations often restart from scratch when the original trial data can't be found.

Change control still happens over email

Approvals, redlines, and sign-offs get buried in threads instead of living on the record they affect, stalling tech transfer while process parameters sit in one scientist's inbox.

Manufacturing works off outdated formulas

The latest revision exists somewhere, just not always where the plant floor can find it in time, and root cause analysis means chasing people instead of querying a system.

No single source of truth for R&D

Disconnected workflows mean information gets lost in handoffs between R&D, quality, and manufacturing, and years of institutional knowledge can walk out the door when a scientist moves on.

The gap between plan and plant

Your PLM has the formula. The plant has the recipe.

Traditional PLM stores an approved formula. How it's actually made lives in a spreadsheet or a binder beside it: sub-recipes, order of addition, mixing and cure conditions. So one base formula becomes ten SKUs nobody can version, a single ingredient swap takes weeks to reach every spec it touches, and the document on the line may not be the one that was approved.
