Uncountable 3.0 — Style Guide

Brand & Product Colors

Raw brand hues — do not flip between themes. Reference by variable name, never by hex.

Semantic Tokens

Flip between light and dark via [data-theme]. Always prefer these over raw brand colors for text and surfaces.

Typography

Poppins for display/heading/body (weights capped at 500), IBM Plex Mono for data.
